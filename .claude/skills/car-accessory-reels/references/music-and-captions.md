# Music and captions

## Music: read this before picking a song

Claude can't download copyrighted songs, and shouldn't pretend to know what is #1 on Reels today. Trends change weekly. Here is what works in practice:

### Option A: Use trending audio inside Instagram (best for reach)
1. Edit the video silent (or with `make_reel.py` without `--music`).
2. Upload to Instagram Reels, tap **Add audio**, search "Malayalam" / the song name, and pick the trending ones (marked with the up-arrow).
3. Trim the song to the drop/hook, then nudge the video so the cuts land on the beat.

Heads-up: on a **business/professional account** Instagram only allows its commercial music library. Popular film songs are often blocked or get muted. If the song is greyed out, switch to Option B or ask Instagram support to confirm. A personal/creator account has wider song access but promoting a shop is still commercial use.

### Option B: Royalty-free / licensed tracks (safe for the shop)
- Instagram's **Sounds for business** (commercial library)
- YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music, Uppbeat, Mixkit (check each licence)
- Search for: "Malayalam mass BGM", "Kerala trap", "Mollywood bass", "mass cinematic", "phonk drift", "bass boosted"
- Mallu-style mass BGMs (slow build, then drop) fit car reveals very well.

### Option C: Original music made for you
AI-generated or commissioned tracks (a local Kerala DJ or producer) in the style of "mass Malayalam BGM": no copyright issues.

### Picking the right type of track
- Hook + build + **drop at ~12-20s**. Put the reveal ON the drop.
- Choose a clearly audible beat. Wobbly rhythm makes beat-sync painful.
- Note the BPM (search "<song> bpm" or use a free tap-tempo site). The script needs it.
- Don't use songs with lyrics over your voiceover or on-screen text.

### Beat-sync recipe (any editor)
1. Drop the track on the timeline, mark the beats (CapCut: **Auto beat**; Premiere: markers).
2. Place cuts on every 2nd beat for fast sections, every 4th for calm ones.
3. Put the reveal and the flash/zoom on the biggest drop.
4. Fade the audio out over the last ~1.2s.

## Captions and hashtags

Write in Malayalam or Manglish plus a bit of English; locals respond better to their own language. Short, one idea, one CTA.

Templates (swap the items):
- `ഹെഡ്‌ലൈറ്റ് മാറ്റിയപ്പോൾ ഇതാണ് വ്യത്യാസം 🔥 Fitting same day! WhatsApp: 98XXXXXXXX`
- `Night drive ഇനി പേടി വേണ്ട 🚗💡 LED projector headlight upgrade. Location: <Town>`
- `360 camera fit ചെയ്താൽ parking ഇത്ര easy ആണ്! 📍<Shop Name>, <Town>`
- `Infotainment upgrade ✅ CarPlay | Android Auto | Reverse camera. Call now 📞`

Hashtags (5-10, mix broad and local): `#caraccessories #carmodification #ledheadlights #360camera #androidstereo #kerala #malayalam #mollywood #carlovers #<town>cars #<shopname>`

Always add: location tag, phone/WhatsApp in caption AND on the last frame, and a pinned comment with the price range if you quote prices.
