# Shot lists per service

Each list is a 20-40s reel. Shoot vertical (9:16), phone at 4K/30 or 1080/60, wipe the lens first, and keep the car clean. Duration in brackets is how long each shot stays on screen after editing.

The universal skeleton (use if the job isn't listed):
1. **Hook** (0-2.5s): the best-looking final result OR the "before" ugliness. Big text.
2. **Before** (2-5s): the old/dull/missing thing.
3. **Process** (5-20s): 4-6 fast shots (tools, panel off, wiring, fitting). Satisfying close-ups.
4. **Reveal** (20-30s): the result switching on, walkaround, drive-away.
5. **CTA** (last 3s): shop name, location, phone / WhatsApp.

---

## Headlight upgrade (LED / projector / laser)
- Hook: night shot, headlights switching ON at the camera, low angle (1.5s)
- Before: old yellow/dim halogen beam on a wall, same car (1.5s)
- Process: headlight removed, projector lens close-up, angel-eye / DRL test on the bench, refit (4-5 shots, ~1s each)
- Reveal: side-by-side wall test (old vs new), DRL/sequential indicator in a dark garage, car rolling toward camera
- Tip: shoot reveal in the dark or at dusk. Headlights look 10x better after sunset, and the grade `night_neon` loves it.

## 360 camera (surround view)
- Hook: the 4-quadrant bird's-eye screen with the car "floating" (1.5s)
- Before: tight parking spot / blind corner (1s)
- Process: camera mounting on front, rear, both mirrors; cable routing; calibration mats laid around car (5 shots)
- Reveal: screen-record style close-up of the display while the car reverses toward a pole; steering-linked guidelines turning
- Tip: film the display with the phone at a slight angle to avoid glare; lower the car interior light.

## Android infotainment / touchscreen
- Hook: screen boot-up animation / logo (1.5s)
- Before: stock tiny radio (1s)
- Process: dash panel trim removal, new frame fitting, wiring harness plug, screen mount (5 shots)
- Reveal: CarPlay / Android Auto, maps, a song playing, finger swipe on screen, steering controls working
- Tip: set screen brightness to ~70% and a colourful wallpaper so it doesn't wash out on camera.

## Horn (e.g. Bosch / dual-tone / air horn)
- Hook: horn blast with a visual punch-in cut on the beat (1s)
- Process: old horn out, new horn mounted behind the bumper, relay wiring (4 shots)
- Reveal: car front-on, press the horn, cut on the sound; optional "before vs after" audio A/B
- Tip: keep the original horn audio for the reveal. Duck the music (-12 dB) for that second.

## Other quick jobs (reversing camera, DRLs, ambient lights, seat covers, PPF, sunfilm, sound system)
Use the skeleton. Always include a hands-at-work shot, a close-up of the finished part, and one wide car shot.

## B-roll to keep in a folder (reuse across reels)
- Shop exterior and signboard at golden hour
- Slow pan of the workshop with tools
- Staff smiling / thumbs-up with the customer's car (with permission)
- Logo end card
